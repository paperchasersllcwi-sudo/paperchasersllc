Paper Chasers LLC is an Appleton-based administrative and litigation support company serving attorneys, businesses, property professionals, families, and individuals across the Fox Valley and throughout Wisconsin.

Professional Service Boundaries

Not a Law Firm

Paper Chasers LLC is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation.

No Legal Advice

We do not advise clients regarding legal rights, strategy, claims, defenses, or legal remedies.

No Guaranteed Results

We cannot guarantee service, filing acceptance, title issuance, pardon approval, court action, or agency decisions.

Client Review Required

Clients remain responsible for factual accuracy, signatures, deadlines, instructions, decisions, and final approval.

When an assignment requires legal advice, legal representation, or independent legal judgment, the client should consult a licensed attorney.
