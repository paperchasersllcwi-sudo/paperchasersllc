How an assignment works

  1. Send the request

    Use the form, call, or text. Tell us the task, location, deadline, and what you need back.

  2. Get scope and price in writing

    We confirm what's included, the timeline, and the cost. Nothing starts until you agree.

  3. We do the work

    You hear from us when anything changes: a failed attempt, a delay, or missing information.

  4. You get the proof

    Affidavits, delivery confirmations, receipts, and finished documents, ready for your file.
