About the show

Michael Winius is a litigation paralegal in Appleton and the owner of Paper Chasers LLC. He has also lived the other side of the system, including supervision and reentry. The People’s Paralegal grew from the questions he hears most from families, people coming home, and people handling a court matter without a lawyer.

Each episode aims to explain not just what the rules are, but why the system works the way it does, so you can read your own paperwork, ask better questions, and know when it’s time to talk to an attorney.
